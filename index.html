Questions

Good to know before you start.

What kind of business is it for?

Lodges, guest houses, hotels, campsites and other places with rooms, a restaurant or a bar. You switch on only the departments you use.

Do I need internet all the time?

No. The app is kept on your device, and recent pages and data stay available when the connection drops. Work done offline is sent when you are back online. Sending email or WhatsApp, and Max, need a connection.

Which devices does it work on?

Any modern phone, tablet or computer with a web browser. You can install it from this page, so there is nothing to download from a store.

How do I install it?

Press "Install the app" on this page. On an iPhone, use Safari, press Share and choose Add to Home Screen. It is not on the App Store or Google Play yet.

Does Max change things on its own?

No. Max prepares each step and shows it to you. It only happens when you press Confirm. Max can be wrong, so always check important figures on the page.

Can I send invoices and booking confirmations to guests?

Yes, by email, WhatsApp or text message, once you connect your own sending accounts under Settings. Until then they wait in the Messages page for you to send by hand.

Does it handle tax and legal requirements?

Bills show tax, and amounts are in NAD. What your business must record, charge and keep is to be confirmed with the relevant legal bodies, authorities and entities of Namibia and with your accountant. We do not claim approval by any authority.

Can I move my existing data across?

Yes. The import tool reads rooms, guests, bookings and stock lists from a spreadsheet or an export from another system, and shows you what it found before anything is saved.

What does it cost?

Pricing is to be announced. Businesses can create an account now and try the app with sample rooms.
