Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026.

This is a plain language summary written for the pilot. It is not legal advice. How privacy and data protection rules apply to your business and to this service is to be confirmed with the relevant legal bodies, authorities and entities of Namibia.

What the service holds

  • Account details of the people who sign in: name, email, a password kept in scrambled form, and the role the owner gave them.
  • The business information each customer enters: rooms, bookings, guest details, orders, stock, staff records and documents.
  • A record of important actions (the audit trail), with who did them and when.

Whose data it is

The business that signs up decides what guest and staff information it enters and why. We use it only to run the service for that business. We do not sell it.

The AI assistant (Max)

Max is optional and is switched on by the owner. When it is on, the question and the information needed to answer it (for example booking figures and the procedures the owner uploaded) are sent to an outside AI service to produce the answer. Guest email addresses and phone numbers are not sent. Answers can be wrong and should be checked.

Sending messages

Email, WhatsApp and text messages are sent through the accounts the business connects. The messages and the contact details needed to send them pass through those providers.

On your device

The app keeps a copy of recent pages and data on the device so it can work offline, and a sign in token so you stay signed in. Signing out removes the saved data.

Your choices

Account holders can ask for their data to be corrected or removed. Guests should ask the business that holds their booking. Removal of records that must be kept for legal or tax reasons is to be confirmed with the relevant authorities.

No promise of perfect security

We work to protect the data, but no system is completely secure, and we do not claim that this one is.
